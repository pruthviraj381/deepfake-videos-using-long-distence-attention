How Long-Distance Attention Deepfake Detection Works

Unlike traditional single-frame CNNs that fail on temporal inconsistencies, our architecture captures long-distance relationships between distant frames across the video.

01
Video Ingestion

Accepts MP4, AVI, and MOV video uploads. Validates video stream integrity, format, and 50MB file size limit.

02
Face Extraction & Crop

OpenCV Haar Cascade detects primary facial landmarks. Crops face with boundary margins to retain manipulation blending seams, resized to 64×64.

03
CNN Frame Encoder

4-layer Convolutional feature extractor with BatchNorm and ReLU transforms each frame into an embedding vector ($d_{model}=64$).

04
Positional Encoding

Learnable temporal positional embeddings encode the sequential order and temporal distance of frames across the entire video timeline.

05
Long-Distance Attention

Multi-Head Self-Attention layers calculate temporal correlation across distant frames, uncovering unnatural warping, blending artifacts, and blinking anomalies.

06
Softmax Classification

Feed-Forward layers and Softmax activation produce verified probabilities for REAL and FAKE categories, complete with confidence percentages.

Academic Excellence

Optimized for Low-Resource Environments (4GB RAM)

Designed to run efficiently on standard consumer laptops without requiring expensive GPU hardware:

  • Pure CPU Execution: PyTorch CPU tensor operations with minimal memory consumption.
  • Long-Distance Uniform Sampling: 4 representative frames sampled across the timeline instead of redundant consecutive frames.
  • 64 × 64 Normalized Resolution: Fast matrix multiplication while preserving crucial facial boundary artifacts.
  • 100% Genuine Predictions: Output generated strictly by genuine forward pass through the trained PyTorch neural network.
